# Privacy Policy for Aypoupen

**Effective Date:** June 30, 2026

Thank you for using **Aypoupen**. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## Information We Collect

### Guest Mode

You may use Aypoupen as a Guest. We do not collect personal account information when you use the app in Guest mode.

## Game Scores

Aypoupen may store game scores and progress on your device to provide features such as score tracking and game progress.

The information stored may include:

* Player name (if provided)
* Game scores
* Progress within the app
* Leaderboard rankings (if applicable)

## Children's Privacy

Aypoupen is designed for children aged 2–7 years.

We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that personal information has been collected in error, they may contact us to request its deletion.

## How We Use Your Information

We use stored information to:

* Save game progress.
* Store and display scores.
* Enable leaderboard and score comparison features (if available).
* Improve the app's stability and performance.

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We do not share your personal information with third parties except where required by law.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

## Data Retention

Game data is kept only as long as necessary to provide the app's features or until it is deleted from the app or your device.

## Third-Party Services

Aypoupen does not use third-party services to collect or process your personal information.

## Your Choices

You may:

* Play the game without creating an account.
* Delete the app and its data from your device at any time.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted within the app or on the page where this Privacy Policy is available.
