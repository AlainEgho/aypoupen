# Privacy Policy for Aypoupen

**Effective Date:** June 30, 2026

Thank you for using **Aypoupen**. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## Information We Collect

### Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with your Google account, we may collect:

* Your Google account display name
* Your email address
* Your Google account unique identifier
* Your profile picture (if available)

This information is used only to identify your account, save your progress, and display your name on scoreboards.

### Guest Mode

You may also use Aypoupen as a Guest. In Guest mode, no personal account information is collected.

## Game Scores

Aypoupen stores game scores and progress using **Firebase**. The information stored may include:

* Player name (if signed in)
* Game scores
* Progress within the app
* Leaderboard rankings

This information is used only to provide game features such as score tracking and friendly competition.

## Children's Privacy

Aypoupen is designed for children aged 2–7 years.

We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that personal information has been collected in error, they may contact us to request its deletion.

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

* Authenticate users through Google Sign-In.
* Save game progress.
* Store and display scores.
* Enable leaderboard and score comparison features.
* Improve the app's stability and performance.

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your information is shared only with trusted service providers that help operate the app, such as:

* Google Authentication
* Firebase (Google Cloud)

These services process data according to their own privacy policies.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

## Data Retention

We keep account information and game scores only as long as necessary to provide the app's features or until you request that your data be deleted, where applicable.

## Third-Party Services

Aypoupen uses the following third-party services:

* Google Sign-In
* Firebase Authentication
* Firebase Firestore or Realtime Database (if applicable)
* Firebase Cloud Storage (if applicable)

These services are provided by Google and are governed by Google's privacy practices.

## Your Choices

You may:

* Play as a Guest without signing in.
* Choose to sign in using your Google account.
* Request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted within the app or on the page where this Privacy Policy is available.
